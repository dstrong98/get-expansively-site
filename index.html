Account controls

Delete your Expansively account

Account deletion is available directly inside Expansively.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to Expansively.
  2. Open Account Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Re-enter your current password.
  5. Type DELETE and confirm.

Open Account Settings

What deletion does

Deletion is intended to permanently remove or anonymize your account and associated personal content, including profile information and user-generated content, except for limited records that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Some backup copies may remain temporarily until normal backup rotation completes.

Before deleting

Account deletion cannot normally be undone. Save any content or information you need before confirming. Deleting the app from your device does not delete your account.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account or the deletion control, contact support@getexpansively.com. We may need to verify account ownership.
