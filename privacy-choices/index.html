Your privacy choices

Use app settings and the options below to manage your account and personal information.

Access and correction

You can review and update supported profile and account fields from within Expansively. For information that cannot be changed in the app, contact the privacy team.

Delete your account

In Expansively, open Account Settings and select Delete Account. You will be asked to re-enter your password and type DELETE. See the Account Deletion page for details.

Device permissions

Camera, photo-library, microphone, notification, and location permissions can be reviewed in your device settings. Disabling a permission may limit features that need it.

Communications

Service messages may be necessary for security, account administration, and requested activity. Where optional promotional communications are offered, use the unsubscribe control or contact support.

Submit a privacy request

Email privacy@getexpansively.com with the request type and your account username. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of certain personal information. Exceptions may apply for security, legal obligations, and the rights of others.
