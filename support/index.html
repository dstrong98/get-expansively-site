Before contacting support

  1. Confirm you are using the latest available version.
  2. Try signing out and back in when safe to do so.
  3. Record the page, action, approximate time, and exact error message.
  4. Remove sensitive information before attaching screenshots.

What to include

Include your username, device type, app or browser version, a concise description of what happened, and steps that reproduce the issue. Never send your password, authentication token, or full payment-card information.

Response time

Response times vary based on request volume, severity, and the information available. Safety and account-access issues may be prioritized.
